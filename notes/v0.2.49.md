### Added

- Real themes. A theme now sets every color of the console, generated from a background, a primary and an accent color, with text, controls and charts kept readable in light and dark mode. Orbit, Tide and High contrast keep their colors as presets, and eight ready-made themes join them.
- A theme editor in Settings > General > Appearance: colors that repaint the console while you drag, light and dark previews side by side, a contrast summary, undo and a name. Fine tuning lists every color with its contrast against what it sits on, and Fix picks the nearest color that reads.
- Share a theme as an `orbit-theme:` code. Anyone can paste it into their Orbit to wear the same colors, in their own mode; the code holds only colors and the name.
- Workspace themes. Administrators give a workspace the theme they are wearing; people who never chose one see it in their own light or dark mode, people with their own can follow it, and switching workspaces switches the theme without a reload. The API returns it with each workspace and sets it with `PUT /workspaces/{workspaceId}/appearance`.
- Wiki templates: Runbook, Decision record, Incident report and Onboarding notes open a new page with its sections ready. Pages render Markdown checklists.

### Changed

- Every color in both consoles comes from the theme, including the mission card, heroes, scenes, illustrations and Ted, whose suit follows the primary color. Choosing a ready theme keeps the colors you had chosen.
- The network exits page has its own scene with the devices online, device cards with what they carry and their traffic, a three step empty state, grant cards with their destinations, and a guide at the end.
- The wiki has its own scene, collections that say who reads and who writes each one, a library with folders, recently edited pages and instant title search, and a reading view with contents and comfortable prose. A collection without folders opens on its library instead of its first page.
- Ted's briefing tells six chapters instead of four, covering servers that form one cloud, shipping from code, an image or the catalog, data that survives, domains, tunnels, exits and status pages, themes and the team, and Ted with AI machines. Each chapter plays on a dark stage with its own animation, pauses while you read, and the last one waits for you to start the mission.
- Settings: secrets sit beside API tokens and login providers, Git providers fit three a row, the system section opens with an index of its parts, and What's new shows the five latest versions with a button for more.

### Fixed

- Stat cards show their icon on the right, centered with the label, and their number at full size again. Since the move to Mavis RC.16, styles for the contents of cards had stopped applying on network exits, backups, AI machines, servers, activity and DNS, and parts of settings, the catalog and the vault pages; the domains and DNS card in Settings squeezed its text into a narrow column.
- Pods reach services on their own host on servers whose image ends the INPUT chain with a reject, as Oracle Cloud's do. Orbit accepted pod traffic only in FORWARD; it now accepts it in INPUT as well, before the image's reject. Orbit and the agent reapply the firewall when they start, so servers fix themselves on update.

## Install or update

On a server with Ubuntu 22.04, 24.04 or 26.04, or Debian 12, on x86_64 or arm64:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/getcortexlabs/orbit-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sudo sh
```

Servers on 0.1.22 or later can also update from Orbit updates in Settings.

Documentation: https://orbit.getcortexlabs.com
