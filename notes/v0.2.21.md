Move a domain to Cloudflare with every step guided, set up several domains and their www in one review, and let your applications send email through the Resend account Orbit uses.

### New

- **Move DNS to Cloudflare.** A domain whose DNS lives where Orbit cannot change it, such as GoDaddy or Squarespace, moves to a new Cloudflare zone in one review. Orbit reads the registrar through RDAP, reads the zone's records at its own nameservers and says which ones it copies, creates the zone and shows the nameservers Cloudflare assigned. The registrar keeps the domain; only its nameservers change.
- **The move, step by step.** Each step turns green on its own: DNSSEC turned off with a countdown for its `DS` record, the nameservers changed with the registrar's own steps for Squarespace, GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger and Registro.br, the registry and three public resolvers giving the new nameservers, the zone active, and HTTPS. Orbit configures the domain once the zone is active, with the dialog open or closed.
- **Several domains in one review.** The application's **Domains** and the **DNS** page say how many domains Orbit can set up now and set them up together, reporting each one. A zone the shared Cloudflare token reaches can be given to the workspace from the domain's dialog, and the dialog reminds you to remove the domain from GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel.
- **www, and redirects.** Adding a root domain offers its `www` companion, with visitors sent to the one you choose and the path kept. A domain that others redirect to cannot be removed while they do.
- **Email for applications.** The **Email** section in an application's **Variables** creates or adopts a sending domain in Resend, publishes its records where Orbit manages the zone, and creates a key that can only send from that domain. The application reads it through `RESEND_API_KEY`, the `SMTP_*` settings and `EMAIL_FROM`, resolved at deploy, and **Rotate key** replaces it with a deploy. n8n, Metabase, Grafana, Directus and Vaultwarden from the catalog get it under their own variable names, and installing one offers it.
- **Deliveries per application.** The Email section shows the last seven days of email sent, delivered, bounced and marked as spam, and the addresses that bounced. A new warning opens when an application nears 4% bounces or 0.08% spam complaints, where Resend starts reviewing the whole account.

### Fixed

- A bounce or spam complaint stops Orbit from writing to an address only when it is about email Orbit sent. Before, a bounce of any email on the Resend account did.

### Upgrading

- No schema migration.
- Moving DNS needs a Cloudflare token with **Zone: Zone: Edit** besides **Zone: DNS: Edit**. A token for a brand new account with no zone yet is accepted.
- Application email needs Resend connected in Settings, under Notifications, Email. Deliveries need Orbit at a public HTTPS address, where Resend reports them.

## Install or update

On a server with Ubuntu 22.04, 24.04 or 26.04, or Debian 12, on x86_64 or arm64:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/getcortexlabs/orbit-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sudo sh
```

Servers on 0.1.22 or later can also update from Orbit updates in Settings.
