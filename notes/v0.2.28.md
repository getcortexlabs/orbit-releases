Review savings and recover your services through guided Mavis journeys.

### New

- **Savings in the console.** Review CPU and memory recommendations backed by at least seven days of measurements, inspect their impact and apply eligible changes sequentially. Follow operations, review their history, configure operating hours and opt into a weekly summary. Estimated capacity shares are distinguished from reductions in the provider bill; consolidation remains advisory.
- **Guided recovery.** Review a server outage, move affected services and DNS records, or set up a Cloudflare Tunnel through the console. Protect databases and Spaces with off-server backups and isolated restore verification.
- **Build repairs and application email.** Review a recognized build failure, apply its suggested configuration fix and retry the same commit, with undo available. Configure receiving addresses, forwarding and alert replies, and reference email credentials from application variables.

### Fixed

- Operations recover their current status when the live event stream falls back to polling, including a first visit to a failed deployment.
- Batch backup protection waits for verified operations and avoids repeating completed items. A failed weekly-summary preference save restores the previous value.

### Upgrading and validation

- No new metadata schema migration. Existing projects, permissions and private fleet policy retain their configuration.
- The maintainer confirmed real production acceptance of a savings recommendation based on at least seven days of measurements and of authenticated replies to alerts. These owner-reported checks are distinct from automated test evidence.

## Install or update

On a server with Ubuntu 22.04, 24.04 or 26.04, or Debian 12, on x86_64 or arm64:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/getcortexlabs/orbit-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sudo sh
```

Servers on 0.1.22 or later can also update from Orbit updates in Settings.

Documentation: https://orbit.getcortexlabs.com
