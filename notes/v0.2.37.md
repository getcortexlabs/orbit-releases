Read, reply and archive from the same inbox view. The message list stays beside the reader on wide screens, and the reader fills the available width on phones.

### Added

- Reply from the selected mailbox or move the open message to the provider's archive folder. Keyboard focus returns to the list when you close or archive a message.
- Switch messages or mailboxes while writing. Each draft stays with its workspace, mailbox, folder and message while the Email page is open. If delivery is uncertain, the draft and its warning stay together so you can check Sent before trying again.

### Changed

- Updated the console to Mavis 0.1.0-rc.15 and Jolt 0.1.0-rc.17.

## Install or update

On a server with Ubuntu 22.04, 24.04 or 26.04, or Debian 12, on x86_64 or arm64:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/getcortexlabs/orbit-releases/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sudo sh
```

Servers on 0.1.22 or later can also update from Orbit updates in Settings.
